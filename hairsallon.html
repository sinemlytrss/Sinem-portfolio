Overview

Luna Hair Studio is a modern business website created for a beauty salon, combining a clean aesthetic with a user-focused experience. Every element was designed to reflect the salon's identity while keeping the interface simple, elegant, and easy to navigate. The goal was to create a professional online presence that builds trust and encourages potential clients to take the next step.
